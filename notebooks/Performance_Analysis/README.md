# 📓 Notebooks

This folder contains all Jupyter notebooks used for systematic technical stock analysis.

The notebooks are modular, well-documented, and designed to be used independently or as part of a complete market performance and risk evaluation pipeline.

Notebooks are numbered to reflect the recommended reading and execution order.

---

## 📂 Notebook Overview

### `01_Chart_Comparison.ipynb`

Compares the historical price performance of the S&P 500 and a selected stock since 2010 using dual-axis charts and historical price data.

---

### `02_AverageReturn_vs_Volatility.ipynb`

Analyzes the risk-return profile of a stock relative to the S&P 500 by computing annualized returns and volatility.

---

### `03_Normalized_Performance_Comparison.ipynb`

Compares the normalized performance of a stock and the S&P 500 to evaluate relative growth and long-term outperformance.

---

### `04_Performance_Animation.ipynb`

Creates an animated visualization of normalized stock and market performance over time to highlight trends and turning points.

---

### `05_Sharpe_Ratio_Comparison.ipynb`

Calculates and compares the annualized Sharpe Ratio of a stock and the S&P 500 to assess risk-adjusted performance.
